## turingmachine.io

The examples of [turing-machine-viz](https://github.com/aepsilon/turing-machine-viz/tree/master/src/examples) (turingmachine.io) by Andy Li, converted to the turingmachinesimulator.com format with the comments and exercises kept. The blank is always `_` (in busy-beaver-3, busy-beaver-4 and copy1s the blank was 0), and the states without rules are the accepting states, except `reject`. Copyright 2015-2021 Andy Li, BSD 3-Clause license; the license text is in [turing-machines/examples/LICENSE-turingmachine-io](turing-machines/examples/LICENSE-turingmachine-io).
